Delete Kitchen Uprising account and data

You can request deletion even if you no longer have the app.

In the game

Open Community > Profile > Delete online data and confirm. This removes your game account, cloud save and crew membership, requests analytics deletion, and records a request to remove remaining leaderboard and social data. Local progress stays on the device. Automatic sign-in stops until you choose to connect again.

Without the game

Email guildstudios676@gmail.com with the subject “Kitchen Uprising data deletion”. Include your player ID or full profile name if you have it, and specify whether you want the whole account or particular data removed. If you cannot find your ID, explain how you used the game so we can help locate it. Do not send passwords or payment-card information.

We may ask for reasonable proof that you own the account. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days. We remove the account, identifying profile/cloud data, crew membership, social relationships and associated ranking records. Limited security, fraud, transaction or legal records may need to be retained; we explain applicable exceptions. Non-identifying aggregate event totals can remain.

Uninstalling alone does not delete online data. Your Google account, Play purchase history and Play Games profile are controlled separately by Google. Manage game-specific Play Games data in the Play Games app/settings. Contact us if you need help. Developer: GuildGames. Game: Kitchen Uprising.

NSMQ Online — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026 (ads added) · Developer: Guild Games · Contact: guildstudios676@gmail.com

NSMQ Online ("the app", "we") is an online quiz game in which two or three schools play live contests against each other. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, who processes it, and the choices you have. The app is meant for players aged 13 and over.

1. Your account

When you open the app it signs you in anonymously through Unity Authentication. This creates a random player ID; we never ask for your real name, email address or phone number to play.

If you choose to sign in with or link Google Play Games, Google shares an identifier for your Play Games account with Unity Authentication so you can keep your progress on a new phone. That sign-in is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

2. What other players can see

You choose a display name and a school. Other players see them in rooms and matches, and on the public leaderboards together with your rating, your weekly points, your school's points and any crest frame you show. Please don't use your real name or anyone else's personal information as your display name.

3. Information we process

InformationWhy
Player ID (anonymous)To identify your account to the online services.
Display name, school, chosen crest frameTo show who is playing, in matches and on leaderboards.

Crash reports and device information (model, OS version)To find and fix crashes.

We do not collect your contacts, photos, files, precise location, microphone or camera. Your sound, voice and accessibility settings stay on your phone.

4. Who processes your information

The online features run on Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies): Authentication, Relay, Lobby/Sessions, Cloud Code, Cloud Save, Leaderboards, Remote Config, Analytics and Diagnostics. Unity processes the information above on our behalf under the Unity Privacy Policy. Purchases are handled by Google Play. Ads are served by Unity Ads (section 5). We do not sell your information and we share it with no one else.

5. Analytics and advertising

Analytics are off unless you turn them on in Settings ▸ "Share anonymous usage data", and you can turn them off at any time, which stops collection. Events are tied only to your anonymous player ID.

NSMQ Online shows ads through Unity Ads (Unity Technologies): a full-screen ad after a match, never during one, and an optional "rewarded" ad you can choose to watch to wear a crest frame for a day. To show and measure ads, Unity Ads receives your device's advertising ID, basic device and network information (including your IP address) and how you interact with the ads; Unity uses it under the Unity Ads privacy policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings ▸ Google ▸ Ads). Buying Remove ads stops the after-match ads. Nothing you can buy helps you win a match.

6. Purchases

When you buy a question pack or a crest frame, Google Play processes the payment; we never see your payment details. The receipt is sent to our server, which asks Google Play to confirm it and then records what your account owns.

7. How long we keep it

  • Your account, record, rating, purchases and leaderboard entries are kept while your account exists.
  • Match verification records are kept for up to 12 months.
  • Analytics events (if you turned them on) are kept for up to 13 months, then deleted or aggregated.
  • The weekly leaderboard resets every Monday.

8. Deleting your account

In the app: Settings ▸ Account ▸ Delete my account. This permanently deletes your account and the data linked to it (profile, record, rating, achievements, leaderboard entries and owned items). Points you already added to your school's total stay in that total, without your name.

You can also ask us by email at guildstudios676@gmail.com with the subject "Account deletion" and your display name and school. We complete requests within 30 days. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete your account.

9. Children

The app is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app, contact us and we will delete the account.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, or to object to its use. Contact us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days.

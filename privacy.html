Kitchen Uprising Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Kitchen Uprising is published by GuildGames (the developer of this game). Contact us at guildstudios676@gmail.com for privacy questions, access, correction or deletion requests. This policy applies to the Android game with package com.GuildGames.kitchenuprising and its support pages.

Data used by the game

The game saves progress, Sauce balances, upgrades, owned products, paint jobs, achievements and preferences on your device. When online services are enabled, Unity Gaming Services assigns a player identifier and stores cloud progress, scores, profile names, crew membership, contributions, friends relationships and related technical information. Your public profile name, score, selected country/region, paint job and crew tag may appear to other players. Country/region is inferred from device locale; the game does not request GPS location.

Google Play Games can provide sign-in and maintain achievements and rankings. Authentication tokens link the game account to that service when configured. Google Play handles payments. The game receives purchase/product identifiers and transaction or receipt information to grant digital items and restore eligible purchases. We do not receive your complete payment-card details.

The game uses Unity LevelPlay with ironSource Ads and Unity Ads for advertising. Advertising services may process device and advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, app interactions and diagnostic information for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and, where permitted and chosen, personalization. Non-personalized ads can still involve data processing. Ad availability and providers depend on the build and region.

Optional Unity Analytics records gameplay events such as completed runs, selected mode, achievements, store visits and purchases. Analytics is linked to pseudonymous identifiers and is not anonymous. It is off unless you enable it in Community > Profile. You can turn it off there at any time. Ad personalization is a separate choice available from the shop privacy control.

If you report a player or contact support, we receive the information you submit and relevant identifiers. Reports are restricted to the operator. Local notifications are scheduled on your device with your permission. The share feature opens your chosen sharing application only when you use it.

Purposes and recipients

We use this information to provide the game and online features, deliver purchases, prevent abuse, respond to support requests and operate the community. Optional analytics helps us understand and improve gameplay. Advertising supports the free game.

Service providers include Unity and Google. Public leaderboard/profile information is visible to other players. Data may be processed outside your country under the safeguards used by the relevant provider. See Unity's privacy policy, ironSource privacy information and Google's privacy policy. We do not ask for your device contacts, microphone recordings, camera images or precise location.

Where applicable, processing relies on providing the requested service, legitimate interests such as security and support, legal obligations, or consent for optional uses. Regional advertising choices, including opt-outs where available, can be changed in the game and device settings. Contact us to exercise applicable access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, objection or sale/sharing opt-out rights. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Retention and deletion

Local saves remain until cleared or the app is uninstalled, subject to device backup settings. Online account and gameplay records are retained while needed to provide the service. Use Community > Profile > Delete online data to delete the game account, cloud save and current crew membership and request removal of remaining associated records. Administrator cleanup of leaderboard archives, social relationships and reports is targeted within 30 days. Some provider security logs, backups or legally required transaction records may be retained for the provider's retention period or as required by law. Aggregate statistics that no longer identify a player may remain.

You can also request deletion without the app by emailing guildstudios676@gmail.com with the subject “Kitchen Uprising data deletion” and your player ID or full profile name. We may ask for proportionate ownership verification. Never send a password or card details. We explain any data that must be retained and why. Deleting the game account does not delete your Google account or Google Play Games history; manage those through Google. Purchases and refund rights are governed by Google Play and applicable law.

Age and security

The game is intended for ages 13 and above and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has supplied such information, contact us so we can investigate and remove it. Local age/parental-consent requirements may differ.

We use provider security controls and limit administrative access. No storage or transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure. The support website has no advertising, analytics scripts or submission database; its host may process ordinary access logs. Email requests are handled by the email provider.

We may update this policy when features or practices change. Material changes will be reflected in the game or public policy page. The date above identifies this version.
